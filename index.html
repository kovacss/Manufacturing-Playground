The Founding of a Hold

Your clan has claimed a cavern beneath the mountain. Dig, smelt and smith your way to the relics that will make the hold legendary.

  1. Dig by hand. Hold the mouse on an ore seam or rock wall. Rock gives stone and opens new ground.
  2. Drill. Place a Delving Drill over a seam. It pushes ore out of the side marked with the gold arrow.
  3. Convey. Drag to lay Conveyors. Goods flow into any building a belt points at, and belts merge from the side.
  4. Smelt. Feed a Smelting Forge ore and coal. Two stone make a brick.
  5. Deliver. Belt goods into the Great Hall. They fill the current tier, and the surplus lands in your stockpile for building.
